How it works

  1. Goal

    Systematically compare fine-tuning strategies for open-source LLMs on domain-specific datasets.

  2. Training

    Fine-tuned Mistral-7B and LLaMA-2 with LoRA adapters and PEFT, training a fraction of the parameters and cutting compute by 60% versus full fine-tuning.

  3. Evaluation pipeline

    MLflow tracks every run and logs BLEU, ROUGE-L and perplexity per checkpoint, with comparison dashboards across configurations.

  4. Prompt benchmarking

    A modular suite tests zero-shot, few-shot and instruction-tuned prompting across NLP tasks, showing where each strategy wins or fails.
